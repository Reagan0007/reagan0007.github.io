Course projects from graduate school — the ones with complete deliverables. Roles are noted for group work.

#### Quantitative & Modeling

- **Multi-asset adaptive grid strategy** (Quantitative Investment, solo) — Bridgewater-style all-weather allocation plus three-layer grids, with EGARCH volatility forecasts for capital weighting and dual-period MACD timing; 21-page deck and design doc.
- **Does technical analysis actually work** (Financial Time Series Analysis, group) — DTW-matched 55-day candlesticks of all SSE 50 constituents to 10 classic patterns, then VAR, Granger causality, impulse responses and variance decomposition on MACD/RSI/ROC/MFI versus returns. Verdict: strict pattern strategies significantly underperform the benchmark.
- **Credit-card default prediction** (Data Science & Business Analytics, solo) — 30,000 samples, 25 features; logistic regression, random forest, GBDT and XGBoost compared, with gradient boosting best at AUC-ROC 0.78.
- **Rollover risk management** (Derivatives, solo) — through the Bank of China crude-oil case: cost-of-carry decomposition of roll spreads, a three-layer hedging ladder, and the Greek derivations.

#### Research & Presentation

- **A-share dividend research** (Advanced Corporate Finance, solo) — CSMAR 1991–2024 full-market statistics (¥2.16tn dividends, 57.68% payout in 2024), plus a 60-year literature review and an identification design.
- **Tianfu Communication valuation** (Financial Statement Analysis, group) — a 42-page report from industry and moat analysis through statement restructuring to a valuation model with Excel.
- **Automated Social Science** (Games and Behavior, group presenter) — presented the paper on LLMs as social scientists and subjects; led the system overview and the seven-module flow.
