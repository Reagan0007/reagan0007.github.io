Course projects from graduate school — the ones with complete deliverables.

#### Quantitative & Modeling

- **Multi-asset adaptive grid strategy** (Quantitative Investment) — Bridgewater-style all-weather allocation plus three-layer grids, with EGARCH volatility forecasts for capital weighting and dual-period MACD timing; 21-page deck and design doc.
- **Does technical analysis actually work** (Financial Time Series Analysis) — DTW-matched 55-day candlesticks of all SSE 50 constituents to 10 classic patterns, then VAR, Granger causality, impulse responses and variance decomposition on MACD/RSI/ROC/MFI versus returns. Verdict: strict pattern strategies significantly underperform the benchmark. [Report](/viewer.html?f=技术分析是否有效——第三组.pdf)
- **Credit-card default prediction** (Data Science & Business Analytics) — 30,000 samples, 25 features; logistic regression, random forest, GBDT and XGBoost compared, with gradient boosting best at AUC-ROC 0.78. [Report](/viewer.html?f=Credit%20Card%20Default%20Prediction%20Based%20on%20Machine%20Learning.pdf)
- **Rollover risk management** (Derivatives) — through the Bank of China crude-oil case: cost-of-carry decomposition of roll spreads, a three-layer hedging ladder, and the Greek derivations. [Report](/viewer.html?f=基于衍生品的展期风险管理.pdf)
- **POMO neural TSP solver** (Deep Learning & LLMs) — dual-model ensemble with adaptive normalization: model A uses DPO-style preference learning plus curriculum learning, model B uses LLM-guided data engineering plus LKH3 expert-trajectory behavior cloning and RL fine-tuning; 0.478% optimality gap on the TSPLIB hold-out set, a 76.7% relative improvement over the baseline. [Report](/viewer.html?f=Dual-Model%20Ensemble%20with%20Adaptive%20Normalization%20for%20Neural%20TSP%20Solvers.pdf)
